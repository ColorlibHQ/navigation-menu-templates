Field notes: Lofoten

Edited 12 minutes ago · 164 words

Day three. The ferry to Reine was cancelled because of wind, so we took the coast road instead and stopped at every pull-in. The light here does something strange after four o’clock: everything turns the colour of weak tea.

Lunch was fish soup in Hamnøy, in a room with six tables and a stove. The owner told us the cod season starts properly in February, when the whole harbour smells of drying racks and diesel.

Tomorrow: the Reinebringen steps if the rain holds off, then the long drive back to Svolvær. Remember to buy film, and to call Ingrid about the cabin keys.
